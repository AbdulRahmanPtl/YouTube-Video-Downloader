Frequently asked questions

Which formats are supported?

Video: MP4, WebM and MKV. Audio: MP3, M4A, Opus and WAV. MP4 offers the widest compatibility across devices.

Why is my preferred resolution missing?

Only resolutions that the uploader has published are listed. Very high resolutions such as 4K are only available on videos that were uploaded in that quality.

Why does preparing a file take a while?

Video and audio are often stored separately and must be downloaded and merged, and audio conversion also takes time. Longer and higher-resolution videos take longer.

Can I download playlists or live streams?

No. VidDrop handles one standard video at a time. Playlists, live streams and videos longer than 3 hours are not supported.

Is this legal?

Download only content that you own, that is in the public domain or licensed for reuse, or that you have permission to save. Downloading other content may violate YouTube's Terms of Service and copyright law.
